  • Contact paths working
  • Page titles and descriptions set
  • Images compressed for speed
  • Keyboard navigation checked
  • Links and social profiles connected
  • Analytics ready when needed
  • Domain and HTTPS connected
  • Client review complete
  • Launch plan shared clearly
